What counts as gross profit

Gross profit is revenue minus UA spend, and nothing else. It is measured on cohorts that are already ROI positive at an agreed retention checkpoint between D30 and D60, fixed at signing. Clean, auditable, and shared monthly.

Our delivery guarantee

If NOXGAMES does not deliver the agreed scope at the agreed date, the delivery payment is dropped in full. You keep the second third. We carry the schedule risk, in writing.

Your UA commitment

Pixel Federation commits a minimum of €500K to UA in the first 3 months after release. If the commitment is not met, NOXGAMES receives one third of the development cost as reparation. Our delivery guarantee, mirrored.
