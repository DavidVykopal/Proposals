The Opportunity

The model is already proven.

This is not risky co-development. You are buying firm knowhow and a market-proven product: the same tower defense core has already shipped twice, under two different IPs and themes, both times with positive and growing ROI. The LEGO Group is the partner we want to repeat it with.

A validated playbook

Our tower defense core has shipped twice: as our own Mecha Fortress and as a spin-off co-published with TinySoft, re-themed for their audience. Two releases, two different IPs and topics, positive and growing ROI both times. This is a repeat of a working formula, not an experiment.

Building is the mechanic

Tower defense is the one mainstream genre where building is the moment-to-moment verb. Place a brick model, watch it hold the line, add more bricks to make it stronger. Build, defend, rebuild is the brand promise and the core loop at the same time. This is a fit, not a re-skin.

Ready to release

The engine, combat systems, meta and live-ops backbone from Mecha Fortress exist today. We re-theme, re-balance and polish. That is why the timeline reads in months, not years.

The rebuild loop

Nothing in this game just disappears. Hit an enemy and it bursts apart into loose bricks. Lose a tower and it collapses into the same pile. Those bricks are the currency: you sweep them up mid-wave and spend them on the next build.

Destruction feeds construction. It is the thing children do with the physical product, turned into an economy, and it gives the genre a reason to look like this that no other IP can claim.

Every tower is a build

Towers are real brick models, not sprites with a level number. Each upgrade visibly adds parts: a wider base, a second barrel, a new roof. Progression you can see across the baseplate.

Minifigure heroes

Recognisable minifigure characters drop in as playable heroes with active abilities, each headlining the campaign of their own theme. Collect, level and swap them between runs.

Live-ops from day one

Events, seasonal waves and remote-configured difficulty tuning ship with the release, powered by our existing platform. Set launches and brand moments become in-game events.

Design note: the theme list and enemy rosters above are directional examples chosen to show the range the core supports. Final theme selection, character usage and campaign design happen together with your team during pre-production, inside your brand guidelines.

The LEGO Group NOXGAMES

Track A: the LEGO Group funds and runs user acquisition with its own teams. The baseline split above applies. Switch to Track B to see the split when NOXGAMES runs UA instead.

What counts as gross profit

Gross profit is revenue minus UA spend, and nothing else. It is measured on cohorts that are already ROI positive at an agreed retention checkpoint between D30 and D60, fixed at signing. Clean, auditable, and shared monthly.

Our delivery guarantee

If NOXGAMES does not deliver the agreed scope at the agreed date, the delivery payment is dropped in full. You keep the second third. We carry the schedule risk, in writing.

Your UA commitment

The LEGO Group commits a minimum of €1M to UA in the first 3 months after release. The number is the same in both UA tracks; only who deploys it changes. If the commitment is not met, NOXGAMES receives one third of the development cost as reparation. Our delivery guarantee, mirrored.

Brand, IP & Safety

Your bricks, handled with care.

Everything we produce from your source material stays yours to use, and everything we ship is built to pass the standards you hold every LEGO product to. Approval is a scheduled step in the plan, not a surprise at the end.

You share sources

The LEGO Group provides source materials used in the game and in marketing: part libraries, minifigure and character references, theme style guides and design standards.

We build the game

NOXGAMES turns them into in-game assets in the chosen art direction: worlds, enemies, towers, heroes, UI and effects, built from authentic part geometry.

Assets flow back

Upon request, everything we create is delivered back to the LEGO Group for further use across your own products and channels. Full source files are available as an add-on.

Brand approval, built into the plan

Every milestone carries a scheduled brand review window. Art direction, character usage, naming and set references are locked with your brand team before production continues, so approval never becomes the thing that slips the date.

Child-safe monetisation

No loot boxes, no randomised paid rewards, no gambling-style mechanics. Transparent priced offers only, with spend limits and a parental gate on purchase. The monetisation design is presented for your sign-off before it is built.

Privacy by design

COPPA and GDPR-K compliant from the first build: no behavioural ad targeting of children, no third-party trackers in child sessions, age-gated analytics and a documented data map delivered with the game.
