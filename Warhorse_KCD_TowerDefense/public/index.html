The Opportunity

Warhorse funds it and approves it. NOXGAMES builds, ships and operates it, without a single person coming off your mainline production.

A validated playbook

Our tower defense core has shipped twice: as our own Mecha Fortress and as a spin-off co-published with TinySoft, re-themed for their audience. Two releases, two different IPs and topics, positive and growing ROI both times. This is a repeat of a working formula, not an experiment.

Bohemia is already a defense game

Kingdom Come is a world of raids, palisades, warbands and sieges. Tower defense lives on exactly that: one place to hold, waves that escalate, an enemy roster that changes with the region. Skalitz, Talmberg and Pribyslavitz are defense scenarios already.

The Concept

Raid. Fortify. Hold the line.

Bohemia in 1403 is a land under raid. Every settlement on the road has to hold against an enemy that fits the place: Cuman riders at Skalitz, mercenary companies at Trosky, a siege train at Kuttenberg. Clear the waves, take the spoils, rebuild, ride on.

What counts as gross profit

Gross profit is revenue minus UA spend, and nothing else. Your UA cashflow is therefore repaid in full before a single euro is split. It is measured on cohorts that are already ROI positive at an agreed retention checkpoint between D30 and D60, fixed at signing. Clean, auditable, and shared monthly.

Our delivery guarantee

If NOXGAMES does not deliver the agreed scope at the agreed date, the delivery milestone is dropped in full. You keep that third and the game still ships. We carry the schedule risk, in writing.

What you never provide

People. No developers, artists, producers or live-ops staff. NOXGAMES runs development, store submission, creative production, live-ops and player support end to end. Alongside the budget, Warhorse commits a minimum of €500K to UA in the first 3 months after release, repaid off the top of revenue. If that commitment is not met, NOXGAMES receives one third of the development cost as reparation, mirroring our delivery guarantee.
