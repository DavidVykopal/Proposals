The 2027 rule

40% of pre-bonus Adjusted EBITDA above $100k, staff at least two thirds, split by the existing seniority and position multipliers, capped at three months' pay each. Paid in two halves, at year close and six months later, to people still employed, and only if the cash reserve stays at 8 months or more.

Conditions before the Dec 15 announcement

  1. Dec 1 forecast: 2026 Adjusted EBITDA at least $200k, otherwise halve the reward to $28k (never to zero).
  2. Cash reserve stays at 9 months or more after the payout (estimated about 9.4).
  3. By Dec 1, management signs the $25k transfer and the 2027 rule, after seeing its own line in all four scenarios.
  4. By Dec 10, a one-pager with a named owner who is not the founder, a written list of allowed EBITDA adjustments (no capitalized development counted as profit), and changes applying only to future years.
  5. Two senior developers read the one-pager and both call it a raise, not a cut.

Watch out

Least-tested assumption: management accepts a cut from $91k to about $29k at Conservative. If management signs without seeing its own numbers, the side deal comes back.

Where they fought

Lump sum or deferred?

Wickman argued deferral; five seats said earned pay gets no strings. Resolved: lump sum plus $25k of management money paid in June 2027 to people still employed.

Fix the management bonus from 2027 or now?

Opened 5–2 for 2027. Graham showed that announcing one pool discloses it anyway; by Round 3 all seven backed disclosure and the $25k transfer.

2027 formula numbers

Floors from $100k to $200k and shares from 15% to 50%. Once management was priced in, five seats raised their numbers; the draft took 40% above $100k. Wickman still prefers $150k.
