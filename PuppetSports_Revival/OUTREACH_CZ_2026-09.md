## Three Brno football clubs now have a "first club" message

Sparta Brno, Zbrojovka and Artis all received the same core pitch on the same day, in one city.

- Sparta Brno were offered a **private 50%**, framed as a reference project.
- Zbrojovka and Artis were offered the **public 25%**, phrased as a race ("prvni, ktery do toho
  pujde"), not as a promise that the slot is theirs.

That distinction is what keeps it honest if they compare, and Brno football clubs do compare. The
25% sits openly on puppetsports.com, so nothing said to Zbrojovka or Artis is private information.
The sentence written to Sparta ("v ceskem fotbale ji nikomu jinemu nenabizime") refers to the 50%
and stays true.

**The exposure is Sparta's 50%, not the 25%.** If Zbrojovka or Artis ever learns that a third Brno
club got half off while they were quoted a quarter, the reference-project reason has to be one you
are willing to say out loud.
