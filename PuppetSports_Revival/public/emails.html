A grassroots project for [Federation] that runs on a phone

Email body

We are NOXGAMES, a Brno studio. Puppet Soccer has 10 million downloads and 4.3 stars, live on Google Play right now, [store link]. It is a cartoon game, no violence, played by children.

Attached is what it looks like as a [Federation] edition.

Three things that usually matter on your side. It is fundable from development and grassroots rather than marketing, because the output is participation and reach among children. Local multiplayer works with no network at all, four players on one phone, so it runs in a school hall or a club day with nothing but the devices already in the room. And we can ship it as a clean edition: no advertising and no purchases, which is usually what makes it approvable.

On safety: the base game has no real-time player-versus-player mode. Competition runs through clans, leagues and leaderboards instead. That is deliberate. It removes the surface that cheating and unmoderated contact normally arrive through.

Entry package is $20,000 and takes four weeks. Worth fifteen minutes?

David

Follow-up, 5 days

Development budgets are usually looking for projects that produce a number to report. This one produces installs, sessions and an age breakdown.
