Competition without the cheaters

The base game ships clans, league play, ladders and leaderboards. Real-time PvP is deliberately left out, because it is the surface cheaters hit first and a leaderboard of impossible scores burns a brand faster than anything. The tech is built, and we add it as an add-on if you want it.

Three things we put in the contract, not in a sales pitch

01

You approve before you pay the rest

Fifty percent at signing. The second fifty only falls due once you have played the halfway build and signed it off. If it is not what you agreed to, it does not ship and you do not pay.
