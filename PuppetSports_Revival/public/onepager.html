One fee per package, excluding VAT, quoted on the call. Half at signing, half only once you have played the halfway build and signed it off. From the Licensed Edition up the game either keeps its advertising and purchases and pays you half of net revenue quarterly, or ships clean, with no ads and nothing to buy. Or split it: bring another company, club or league into the same edition and the fee divides between you.
