# Puppet Sports Revival — how to approach partners, and who to approach first

Written 23 Aug 2026. Sources at the bottom. This is the internal working doc; the offer pages
in `public/` are what actually goes out.

---

## 1. The one thing that changes everything about our outreach

**Our games are live and free.** Nobody else pitching a licensed game can say that. The whole
industry sends decks about a game that does not exist yet, asking for money up front to build it.
We can send two sentences and a store link, and the person on the other end is looking at our
product ninety seconds later, with our boards, our cup and our crowd already in it.

That means: **stop thinking "proposal", start thinking "demo".**

The single highest-converting move, and it is cheap for us:

> Before the first email, take the prospect's crest and kit, and mock up **one** Puppet Soccer or
> Puppet Ice Hockey screenshot with their boards, their colours and their name on the cup.
> Attach that image. Nothing else.

That is a thirty-to-sixty minute art job per prospect. Everyone in sponsorship gets decks. Almost
nobody gets a picture of their own club already inside a game that has ten million downloads.
Make this the standard first touch, not an exception.

## 2. Three different buyers, three different sales

They are not the same conversation. Do not send the same page to all three.

| | **Brands** | **Clubs** | **Leagues, federations, associations** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who signs | Head of Sponsorship / Brand Activation Manager, with the CMO | Commercial Director or Head of Partnerships; sometimes Marketing Director alone | Secretary General signs, but the idea comes from Marketing or Development |
| Budget it comes from | Brand activation / campaign budget, sits next to media spend | Marketing, or "digital & fan engagement" | **Grassroots and development**, not marketing. This is the important one. |
| What they actually want | Dwell time and something to talk about. They are bored of boards. | Something for the fans who never buy a ticket, and new inventory to sell | Kids. Participation numbers. Something to show the ministry or the confederation. |
| Our angle | "Your board impression lasts one second. A match lasts four minutes." | "You get inventory you can resell to your own sponsors and keep 100%." | "The cheapest first touch with a ten-year-old you will ever buy." |
| Page to send | `index.html` (core) | `soccer.html` or `hockey.html` | `soccer.html` or `hockey.html` |

**The federation grassroots angle is underrated.** UEFA's HatTrick programme distributes about
**€195 million a year across 55 member associations** for development including grassroots, and
associations are permanently looking for projects that count. A branded Puppet Soccer edition
aimed at kids is fundable out of a pot that is not the marketing budget and not fought over the
same way. Same logic on the hockey side with federation development budgets.

## 3. Why the $20K tier is the whole strategy

At most mid-size clubs and federations, anything under roughly €25K can be signed by a commercial
or marketing director **without a tender, a board paper or procurement**. Above that it becomes a
process that takes two quarters.

So the Sponsor Drop at **$20,000 is not the cheap option, it is the door**. Sell that first, get
the logo on the boards, get a report with real numbers on it, and the Branded Team at $60K becomes
a renewal conversation with an incumbent instead of a cold sale. Do not lead with $120K or $250K
to anyone who has not already bought something from us.

---

## 3b. The lever that makes the big bundles reachable: let them split it

A single mid-size club cannot sign off $120,000. Four of them at **$34,000 each** can, and that is
the same edition with four crests in it. The pages now carry this as a formal term: **a partner can
bring in another club, league, federation or brand and split the fee**, everyone contracts and
invoices separately, and the one who made the introduction keeps the billing.

Use it deliberately, because it changes who you call:

- **Lead with it when a club says the number is too big.** It converts a no into a smaller yes
  instead of ending the conversation. Nobody has to go back to a board.
- **It makes leagues the best prospect on the list.** A league does not have to fund anything
  itself; it introduces four of its clubs and the edition happens. That is a phone call, not a
  budget line, which is a much easier thing to ask a league for.
- **It turns a club into our sales channel.** A club that wants the edition but cannot fund it
  alone has every reason to go and find its own shirt sponsor. They know their sponsors and we do
  not, and they are far more persuasive with them than a cold email from Prague ever will be.
- **Guardrail to state early:** the partner they bring cannot be a competitor inside their own
  exclusive category, and from the third identity onward each one adds $8,000 to the total before
  the split. Say this up front so nobody arrives with twelve clubs expecting $10,000 each.

## 4. Timing: when to hit them

The sponsorship industry's own rule of thumb: **engage 8 to 9 months before the season**, expect
the negotiation itself to take about **6 months**, and know that budgets are typically planned
**2 to 4 months before the fiscal year** with approvals landing **4 to 8 weeks** before it starts.

Applied to us:

| Target | Season | Pitch window | Sign by | Live for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ice hockey clubs and federations (Europe) | Sep to Apr | **Now to Dec 2026** | Feb 2027 | Sep 2027 season start |
| The May 2027 IIHF Worlds window | May | **Now to Nov 2026** | Dec 2026 | 8 to 12 weeks of production fits comfortably |
| Football clubs (Europe) | Jul to May | **Now to Feb 2027** | Apr 2027 | Jul 2027 season start |
| Brands | Calendar year | **Sep to Nov 2026** for 2027 budgets | Dec 2026 | Whenever the campaign needs it |

We are in the right month for hockey and slightly early for football, which is fine. Our
production time (4 to 20 weeks) is short enough that we can also catch mid-season activations that
nobody else can deliver, and that is worth saying out loud in the pitch.

## 6. The routing map — and one gate to know about

- **National federations sell their own rights directly.** Czech, Danish, Slovak, Norwegian,
  Latvian, Polish, Hungarian ice hockey federations are all approachable directly. Same for
  football associations.
- **The IIHF is not.** Infront holds IIHF media and marketing rights **through 2033**, covering the
  World Championship and the Division I tournaments, plus the Champions Hockey League. Anything at
  IIHF level goes through Infront, which means a bigger, slower deal. Do not waste the first six
  months there. Go national first, then use a signed national federation as the reference that
  gets Infront to take a call.
- **Clubs vary.** Big clubs sell through agencies (SPORTFIVE, Infront, Two Circles). Mid-size clubs
  mostly sell in house, which is another reason mid-size is the right target for us.

## 7. What the first email looks like

Short. No attachment except the mockup image. No deck. Subject line is the club's own name.

> **Subject: Sparta on the boards**
>
> Hi [name],
>
> We are NOXGAMES, a Prague studio. Our game Puppet Ice Hockey has five million downloads and 4.8
> stars, and it is live on Google Play right now: [link]. Install it and look at the boards around
> the rink. They say NOXGAMES.
>
> Attached is what they look like saying Sparta.
>
> We are reviving the Puppet Sports franchise and taking one partner per sport. The entry package
> is $20,000 and takes four weeks. Worth fifteen minutes?
>
> David

Then the sport page as the follow-up link once they reply, not before.

**Sequence:** mockup email → 5 working days → short follow-up with one number from the page
(4.8 stars, or 467,000 ratings) → 10 days → LinkedIn message to the same person → park for a
quarter. Three touches, then stop. Keep a simple tracker; do not improvise this.

### Do not start here

- **Top leagues and their clubs.** Premier League, NHL, Bundesliga, the big five. Agency-gated,
  twelve-month cycles, and our whole advantage (speed, one decision maker, a cheque under the
  tender threshold) disappears.
- **The IIHF directly.** See the Infront gate above.
- **Betting brands.** Tipsport, Fortuna, Niké and the rest are the biggest sponsors in Czech and
  Slovak hockey and football, and they would probably say yes. But a betting logo on the boards
  breaks the family rating that the entire pitch rests on, and forces an 18+ store rating that
  costs us the audience. If a club's inventory is betting-dominated, sell them the **Branded Team**
  tier instead and keep the boards on the club itself.

## 9. Two things to fix before the first email goes out

1. **The 42 million number.** Everything else on the pages is verified from Google Play. The
   franchise total is Jiri's figure and it now appears in the hero, the stat strip and the term
   sheet. Find the source (old Play Console exports, App Annie or Sensor Tower history) before a
   partner's analyst asks, because one of them will.
2. **Player likenesses.** Puppet Soccer ships puppet heads modelled on recognisable footballers. A
   club or federation will notice, and some will ask whether they can have their own squad's faces.
   The answer is yes, **with their clearances, not ours**. The term sheet already says this. Do not
   let it be promised loosely in a call.
