# First-contact email templates by client archetype

Five archetypes: **brand**, **gear manufacturer**, **team**, **league**, **association**.
Companion to `OUTREACH.md`, which holds the strategy, the routing map and the lead list.
This file holds the copy you actually paste.

## How to use these

- **No greeting line.** Every template starts on the first substantive sentence. David adds
  his own "Hi [name]," when pasting. Do not reinstate a greeting when editing these.
- **One attachment: the mockup image.** No deck, no PDF, no link tree. The mockup is their
  crest or their product inside a real screenshot from the live game.
- **The link goes to the live store listing first, not to the proposal site.** Getting them to
  install the real game and see the NOXGAMES boards is the whole trick. The proposal page is the
  follow-up once they reply.
- **Subject line is their own name**, not ours. "Sparta on the boards" beats anything about NOXGAMES.
- **Sequence:** mockup email → 5 working days → short follow-up with one number → 10 days →
  LinkedIn message to the same person → park for a quarter. Three touches, then stop.
- Replace every `[square bracket]`. If a bracket is still there, the email is not ready.

## 1. Brand

**Who:** Head of Sponsorship or Brand Activation Manager, signed off with the CMO.
**Budget:** brand activation, sits next to media spend.
**Angle:** a board impression lasts one second, a match lasts four minutes.
**Send them:** `index.html`.

> **Subject: [Brand] on the boards, for four minutes instead of one second**
>
> We are NOXGAMES, a Brno studio with over 250 million downloads across our games. Puppet Soccer
> is one of them: 10 million installs, 4.3 stars, live on Google Play right now — [store link].
> Install it and look at the boards around the pitch. They say NOXGAMES.
>
> Attached is what they look like saying [Brand].
>
> A stadium board gets you a second of peripheral vision. A match in this game holds a player for
> four minutes, and they play it again tomorrow. We are reviving the franchise and taking **a
> maximum of four partners per game**, each one exclusive in their own category, so no competitor
> of yours turns up alongside you.
>
> Entry package is $20,000 and takes four weeks. Worth fifteen minutes?
>
> David

**Follow-up (5 days):** one number, nothing else. "467,000 people have rated Puppet Soccer. The
average sponsored post reaches fewer. Still worth fifteen minutes?"

## 2. Gear manufacturer

**Who:** Brand Manager or Sports Marketing Manager. Sometimes product marketing rather than sponsorship.
**Budget:** brand or product marketing.
**Angle:** everyone else buys a logo on a wall. You get the thing the puppets actually play with.
**Send them:** `index.html`, then the sport page matching their category.

> **Subject: Your [sticks / boots / kit] in a game with over 100 million downloads**
>
> We are NOXGAMES, a Brno studio with over 250 million downloads across our games. Puppet Ice
> Hockey is one of them: 5 million installs, 4.8 stars, live right now — [store link].
>
> Every puppet in that game is holding a stick. Right now it is a generic one. Attached is what it
> looks like as a [product line].
>
> This is the part that is hard to buy anywhere else: for a gear brand the equipment is not
> decoration, it is the thing the player uses to score. Kids learn what your gear looks like before
> they are old enough to ask for it. We can go further and tie in-game items to your real product
> lines, and hand out redeemable codes inside the game that get claimed in your shop.
>
> We take **a maximum of four partners per game**, each exclusive in their category, so you would be
> the only [equipment / apparel] brand in there. Entry package is $20,000 and takes four weeks.
>
> Worth fifteen minutes?
>
> David

**Follow-up (5 days):** "The in-game gear is the one sponsorship slot that survives being muted,
skipped or ad-blocked, because it is the game object itself."

## 3. Team

**Who:** Commercial Director or Head of Partnerships. Sometimes the Marketing Director alone.
**Budget:** marketing, or digital and fan engagement.
**Angle:** inventory you can resell to your own sponsors and keep 100% of.
**Send them:** `soccer.html` or `hockey.html`.

> **Subject: [Club] on the boards**
>
> We are NOXGAMES, a Brno studio. Our game Puppet Ice Hockey has five million downloads and 4.8
> stars, and it is live on Google Play right now — [store link]. Install it and look at the boards
> around the rink. They say NOXGAMES.
>
> Attached is what they look like saying [Club].
>
> The crest goes on the kit, the boards and the cup. The part most clubs care about more: the
> sponsor boards inside your edition are **yours to resell to your own sponsors**, and you keep all
> of it. It is new inventory rather than another cost line, and it reaches the fans who follow you
> but never buy a ticket.
>
> Entry package is $20,000 and takes four weeks. Worth fifteen minutes?
>
> David

**Follow-up (5 days):** "One of your existing sponsors probably already pays for a board that gets
looked at for one second. This one gets played with for four minutes."

## 4. League

**Who:** Commercial or Marketing Director. The idea usually starts there.
**Budget:** commercial, or a central fund shared with clubs.
**Angle:** your season structure is already the game's structure. Your table, your fixtures, your derby week.
**Send them:** `soccer.html` or `hockey.html`.

> **Subject: [League] as a season mode**
>
> We are NOXGAMES, a Brno studio. Puppet Soccer has 10 million downloads and 4.3 stars from
> 467,000 ratings, live on Google Play right now — [store link].
>
> Attached is what it looks like with [League] inside it: your clubs, your table, your fixtures.
>
> The reason this fits a league better than a single club is that the game already runs on seasons,
> ladders and leaderboards, and we ship group stages, straight knockout and elimination tournaments
> out of the box. Your competition structure drops straight in, and derby week becomes an in-game event. Every club in your league is in one product instead of each doing their own thing.
>
> We take **a maximum of four partners per game**, each exclusive in their category. A league
> normally takes the whole edition rather than one slot.
>
> Bundles run $20,000 to $250,000 depending on how much of the league goes in. Worth a call?
>
> David

**Follow-up (5 days):** "Local multiplayer means four kids can play your league on one phone with no
network. That is what makes it work at a school or a fan zone."

## 5. Association / federation

**Who:** the idea comes from Marketing or Development. The Secretary General signs.
**Budget:** **grassroots and development, not marketing.** This is the important difference.
**Angle:** the cheapest first touch with a ten-year-old you will ever buy, and it counts as participation.
**Send them:** `soccer.html` or `hockey.html`.

> **Subject: A grassroots project for [Federation] that runs on a phone**
>
> We are NOXGAMES, a Brno studio. Puppet Soccer has 10 million downloads and 4.3 stars, live on
> Google Play right now — [store link]. It is a cartoon game, no violence, played by children.
>
> Attached is what it looks like as a [Federation] edition.
>
> Three things that usually matter on your side. It is fundable from **development and grassroots
> rather than marketing**, because the output is participation and reach among children. **Local
> multiplayer works with no network at all**, four players on one phone, so it runs in a school hall
> or a club day with nothing but the devices already in the room. And we can ship it as a **clean
> edition: no advertising and no purchases**, which is usually what makes it approvable.
>
> On safety: the base game has **no real-time player-versus-player mode**. Competition runs through
> clans, leagues and leaderboards instead. That is deliberate. It removes the surface that cheating
> and unmoderated contact normally arrive through.
>
> Entry package is $20,000 and takes four weeks. Worth fifteen minutes?
>
> David

**Follow-up (5 days):** "Development budgets are usually looking for projects that produce a number
to report. This one produces installs, sessions and an age breakdown."

## Notes for whoever edits this next (human or agent)

- The **partner cap changed**: it used to read "one partner per sport" everywhere. It is now
  **up to 4 partners per game, each category exclusive**. If you find the old wording anywhere in
  this repo, it is stale.
- **Never promise real-time PvP in a first email.** It is an add-on, quoted per edition, and it is
  deliberately absent from the base game. For associations the absence is a selling point, so do not
  quietly reintroduce it.
- The **250M+ figure is studio-wide across all NOXGAMES games**. The **100M+ figure is the Puppet
  Sports lineup only**. Do not merge them into one sentence, they answer different questions.
- Contact address for this project is `studio@noxgames.com`.
- Templates are English. Czech versions need vykání throughout and no em-dashes.
