Who logged Voldemort hours

1,596 h logged to the Voldemort task in August and September, all by the 8 people below. Nothing was logged to it in July. The other 7 people who track time logged 0 h to it, so company-wide time is not counted here. Hours only; individual rates are deliberately left out.

Tracking gaps to keep in mind. Q3 has 5,192 tracked hours company-wide, and 1,681 h of them are timers with no task at all (mostly people who log everything as "no project"). If some of that was Sector Defense work, the tracked-hours methods undercount it. The focus-share method covers that gap by design. Data as of 23 Sep 2026.
