# Rise of a Nation

## Collaboration Proposal Source Brief

**Prepared by:** NOXGAMES  
**Project type:** British-Indian collaboration for a youth-focused civic strategy game  
**Primary market:** India  
**Target platforms:** Mobile and web  
**Strategic moment:** India's independence anniversary and the Viksit Bharat 2047 vision  
**Indicative budget:** USD 400,000-600,000  
**Indicative production window:** Development start in Q4 2026; target release around the end of Q1 or beginning of Q2 2027  
**Early proof point:** A focused MPP/prototype can be produced in approximately 3-4 weeks

---

## Executive Summary

Rise of a Nation is a next-generation strategy game in which young players do not simply play a leader: they become one.

The game invites players to build the India they want to see in 2047. Starting with a single district and limited resources, they make meaningful choices across infrastructure, education, agriculture, technology, energy, healthcare, employment and the economy. Their district becomes a city, their city contributes to a state, and their decisions ultimately shape the nation.

The opportunity is structured as a British-Indian collaboration connected to India's independence anniversary and the longer Viksit Bharat 2047 vision.

The intended experience combines:

- the strategic clarity and progression of mobile civilization games;
- the persistent city and economy building associated with games such as Clash of Clans and Travian;
- short, accessible sessions suitable for younger players;
- long-term progression that rewards planning and return play;
- civic learning delivered through decisions and consequences rather than lessons;
- an optimistic, modern vision of India based on innovation, national goals and future opportunity.

NOXGAMES can deliver the concept through two viable production routes. The first adapts the proven Hexapolis foundation to reach market faster and reduce technical risk. The second builds a bespoke web-first strategy product around the client's full dashboard and multi-city management vision. Both routes can use NOXGAMES' experience in turn-based, civilization-like mobile games, procedural systems, progression design and sustainable live gameplay.

## Product Vision

### Player promise

> Start with one district. Build a thriving city. Shape a state. Help a nation rise.

The fantasy is constructive leadership. The player creates a customized avatar, chooses a leadership path, and experiences the consequences of decisions made over time.

Possible leadership identities include:

- Architect
- Innovator
- Infrastructure Strategist
- Entrepreneur
- Climate Strategist

The tone should inspire ownership, achievement, national identity and civic responsibility without becoming a history lesson or a political simulation. The setting is contemporary and near-future India, not historical India.

### Audience

The primary audience is children and young people. The experience should:

- be immediately understandable on a phone;
- use short, rewarding play sessions;
- make complex national systems feel approachable;
- show visible cause and effect;
- encourage strategic thinking and balanced growth;
- create pride through building, improvement and collaboration;
- remain engaging over a longer progression arc.

### Long-term message

The player learns that national progress is interconnected. Investing only in one sector is not enough. Education needs employment. Technology needs energy and skills. Agriculture needs infrastructure and water. Economic growth must remain compatible with citizen happiness and sustainability.

The game turns this principle into play: every decision changes the district, its people and the wider nation.

## Core Gameplay

### The impact loop

1. **Start** - The player receives one district, a limited budget and a population with unmet needs.
2. **Build** - The player improves transport, clean energy, AI-enabled agriculture, digital healthcare, schools, research and other systems.
3. **Grow** - Jobs are created, the economy improves, citizen trust rises and new regions or capabilities unlock.
4. **Feel** - The player experiences ownership, achievement, civic responsibility and visible national progress.

This loop must work in short sessions while feeding a persistent metagame.

### Strategic balance

The central design challenge is not simply choosing what to build, but balancing connected national priorities.

Representative systems include:

- infrastructure;
- agriculture and food security;
- education and talent;
- technology and research;
- health and citizen wellbeing;
- energy and sustainability;
- employment and industry;
- finance and economic output;
- citizen trust and happiness;
- state and national contribution.

Each system should create clear benefits, trade-offs and dependencies. For example, rapid industrial growth can increase jobs and revenue but may require more energy, education and environmental investment.

### Mission examples

Client-proposed national missions include:

- **Bullet Train Corridors** - connect cities at record speed;
- **Semiconductor Hubs** - power the digital economy;
- **Drone Farming** - improve agriculture through AI and automation;
- **Renewable Energy Grids** - lead the clean-energy transition;
- **Space Programmes** - develop high-ambition national capability;
- **AI Universities** - build the talent of tomorrow.

Further initiatives, technologies, characters and messages will be defined with the client during discovery.

### Competition and social potential

The concept can support state-versus-state competition based on meaningful national metrics. Players may compare:

- state progress;
- citizen happiness;
- innovation;
- economic output;
- sustainability;
- mission completion;
- overall contribution to national goals.

Social features can be introduced as part of the core scope or a later phase. Potential additions include leaderboards, friend comparisons, cooperative national events, state teams and shared milestones.

### AI cabinet assistant

An in-game cabinet assistant can explain systems, policies and economic consequences in simple language. Its role is to make governance and national development understandable and fun.

The implementation, content controls, age suitability, data sources and use of generative AI must be agreed during discovery. A deterministic authored guide may be preferable for the initial release if compliance, localization or content-review requirements make live generative features unsuitable.

## Production Route 1: Hexapolis Evolution

### Summary

Adapt the existing Hexapolis foundation into a modern, India-focused strategy game.

Hexapolis already provides a proven turn-based, hex-based civilization framework. In the current game, the player enters a world, establishes and grows cities, constructs buildings, gathers resources, creates armies, expands and completes a world. That loop can be reframed around constructive regional development and the expansion of a modern India.

### Proposed experience

- Players progress through Indian regions represented as replayable strategic worlds.
- A first district or city becomes the starting point for each campaign.
- Construction, resources, technology and expansion are reskinned and redesigned around modern national development.
- Progress across individual matches contributes to a persistent map and long-term national journey.
- The game can evolve from smaller regional sessions toward a larger India-scale metagame.
- Combat can be reduced, abstracted or replaced with civic challenges depending on the final audience and content direction.

### Technology

- Unity;
- reuse of relevant Hexapolis architecture and systems;
- established turn-based and hex-grid technology;
- existing procedural generation and strategy-game know-how;
- native mobile deployment.

### Benefits

- fastest path to a playable product;
- lower technical and production risk;
- proven mobile strategy foundations;
- more predictable performance and delivery;
- more production capacity directed toward India-specific art, content and progression;
- stronger fit for the proposed early-2027 release window.

### Trade-offs

- the client vision must be shaped around the strengths of the existing framework;
- the result will be closer to a turn-based civilization game than a detailed persistent city dashboard;
- some Clash of Clans or Travian-style systems may need to be simplified or moved into the metagame;
- extensive changes to the core loop can reduce the advantages of reuse.

### Best fit

Choose this route when deadline confidence, mobile quality and budget control are more important than reproducing every element of the initial dashboard vision.

## Production Route 2: Bespoke Nation Builder

### Summary

Create a new persistent strategy game designed specifically around the client's city, state and national management vision.

The player begins with a small town or district, invests across connected sectors and eventually manages multiple cities, regions and national initiatives. The product can more closely resemble a mobile-accessible civic dashboard with the depth of a long-term builder.

### Proposed experience

- persistent city and region development;
- multiple cities managed in parallel;
- connected sector investment and dependencies;
- technology and policy choices;
- state and nationwide progression;
- long construction and improvement arcs;
- a large strategic dashboard;
- short actions that accumulate into long-term national outcomes;
- direct support for state competition and social systems.

### Technology

- Three.js-based web experience;
- web-first architecture;
- native mobile packaging and device support through Capacitor;
- reuse of NOXGAMES algorithms, procedural methods and genre knowledge;
- back-end services selected during technical discovery.

### Benefits

- closest match to the client's original vision;
- systems and interface designed specifically for the subject;
- strong accessibility across desktop and mobile web;
- flexible content updates and campaign activation;
- better foundation for a persistent multi-city dashboard.

### Trade-offs

- more product and technical discovery is required;
- greater implementation risk than adapting a shipped game;
- native mobile quality, performance and device behavior require dedicated validation;
- more systems must be built and balanced from the ground up;
- scope discipline is essential to protect the early-2027 target.

### Best fit

Choose this route when vision fidelity and persistent management depth are more important than maximizing reuse.

## Commercial Framework

### Indicative development budget

**USD 400,000-600,000**

The current estimate is intentionally a range. Both production routes are expected to sit within a broadly similar commercial envelope, but the allocation of effort differs:

- Route 1 directs more budget toward adaptation, content, art and progression on a proven base.
- Route 2 directs more budget toward new core systems, interface, platform engineering and technical validation.

A fixed price, milestone structure and exact deliverables can only be confirmed after discovery and selection of the production route.

### Execution and payment plan

The proposed engagement proceeds in two contractual stages:

1. **Preliminary agreement and USD 40,000 prepayment** - the preliminary contract defines the prototype objective, delivery window, client inputs, review process and preliminary ownership terms. The prepayment triggers prototype production.
2. **Prototype delivery** - NOXGAMES delivers the agreed MPP/prototype in approximately 3-4 weeks.
3. **Final scope approval and development contract** - both parties use the prototype findings to confirm the production route, release scope, acceptance criteria, deadline and final agreed price.
4. **USD 100,000 production prepayment** - signing the final development contract triggers the second prepayment, team reservation and full production.
5. **Final delivery and balance payment** - following delivery and contractual acceptance, the remaining agreed project price becomes payable.

Both prepayments are intended to be credited against the final agreed project price. Within the current USD 400,000-600,000 indicative range, the final balance would therefore be approximately USD 260,000-460,000.

### Release deadline and price reduction

NOXGAMES is open to committing to a concrete final release deadline in the development contract and negotiating a price reduction if NOXGAMES misses that deadline.

A typical discussion would be a **10-20% reduction**, with the exact percentage and mechanism agreed in the final contract. The contract should also define:

- the deadline and delivery milestone against which delay is measured;
- any grace period;
- whether the reduction is calculated against the full project price or an affected milestone;
- the maximum aggregate reduction;
- written change-control treatment for added or modified scope;
- exclusions for delayed client inputs or approvals;
- force majeure and third-party dependency treatment;
- acceptance procedure and payment due dates.

This is an illustrative commercial structure rather than binding legal language. Tax treatment, intellectual-property transfer, remedies, exclusions and liability caps must be confirmed in the executed agreements.

### Early proof point

NOXGAMES can produce a focused MPP/prototype in approximately **3-4 weeks**.

The proof point should validate the most important unknowns, such as:

- the intended visual direction;
- one representative build-and-balance loop;
- the district-to-nation fantasy;
- mobile usability;
- one national mission;
- the technical direction for the selected route.

### Indicative timing

- **Now / pre-production:** client inputs, route selection, creative and technical discovery;
- **3-4 weeks from approval:** focused MPP/prototype;
- **Q4 2026:** full production can begin, subject to contracting and team reservation;
- **Q1 2027:** core production, integration, content and testing;
- **End of Q1 / beginning of Q2 2027:** realistic release target for a disciplined initial scope.

The schedule is an initial feasibility view, not a committed delivery plan. It depends on timely route selection, content approvals, scope discipline, localization requirements and client feedback.
