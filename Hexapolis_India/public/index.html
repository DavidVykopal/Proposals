The player promise
You do not play a leader. You become one.

The player creates an avatar and chooses how they want to contribute: architect, innovator, infrastructure strategist, entrepreneur or climate strategist. The role is an identity and progression lens, not a restriction. Every player must still understand that national progress depends on balanced choices.

Strategic thinkingDecisions have visible national consequences.
Innovation mindsetEmerging technology solves practical problems.
Productive playPurpose is delivered through systems, not lessons.
Leadership skillsOwnership grows from district to nation.

Strategic depth

Every system must depend on another

The game becomes interesting when there is no single correct investment. Education creates skilled workers, but workers need jobs. Industry creates revenue, but requires energy and transport. Growth increases demand on health, housing and food.

Design rule: every major investment creates one clear benefit, one new dependency and one measurable effect on citizens.

Content framework

National goals become playable missions

Missions translate broad ambitions into concrete objectives, dependencies and outcomes. Each can become a regional scenario in Variant 1 or a persistent multi-stage programme in Variant 2.

01
Highest delivery confidence

Hexapolis Evolution

Reuse the proven Unity, hex-grid and turn-based foundation, then redirect it from conquest toward constructive regional development.

The product shape

Short regional strategy sessions. One persistent national journey.

Each playable world becomes a district or regional challenge. The player explores a hex map, establishes services, develops resources, deploys specialist teams and completes a national mission. Results feed a persistent India progression layer between sessions.

  • Target session: approximately 8-12 minutes for a complete regional objective.
  • Core mode: turn-based planning on a compact hex map, designed for one-handed mobile play.
  • Conflict replacement: military pressure becomes crises, constraints, logistics and civic challenges.
  • Persistence: technologies, leadership specialization and national milestones survive between worlds.
The reusable foundationHex maps, exploration, settlement growth, turn resolution and native mobile delivery already exist.

Production breakdown

Reuse the engine. Rebuild the meaning.

Route 1 is not a reskin. Its advantage comes from retaining low-level production infrastructure while replacing the player-facing economy, content and progression.

Foundation retained and adapted

  • Unity project, device support and native deployment pipeline
  • Hex-grid world representation and map-generation methods
  • Camera, touch input, selection and core map interaction
  • Turn sequencing, action resolution and deterministic game state
  • Building placement, territory expansion and map visibility
  • Save architecture, profile foundations and analytics hooks
  • AI and scenario framework where it supports civic challenges

Product rebuilt for Rise of a Nation

  • All modern India environments, buildings, characters and UI
  • Resources: budget, workforce, energy, knowledge and trust
  • Sector dependencies, research tree and national missions
  • Specialist teams replacing conventional military units
  • Civic events, regional objectives and non-combat victory rules
  • Avatar roles, persistent national map and leadership progression
  • Age-appropriate onboarding, advisor, localization and content

The reuse boundary

When this route stops being the faster route

The Hexapolis advantage disappears if the core requirement becomes a continuously running multi-city economy. That changes the state model, interface, progression and content pipeline deeply enough that a bespoke foundation becomes more honest.

Good fitTurn-based regional scenarios, compact mobile sessions and a persistent layer between matches.
Poor fitAlways-on construction timers, simultaneous management of many cities and dashboard-first play.
Delivery implicationHighest confidence for early 2027 only if the product remains inside the regional-session model.
02
Highest vision fidelity

Bespoke Nation Builder

Build the client's persistent city, state and nation-management vision directly, without constraining it to a match-based hex game.

The product shape

A living command center for India's future.

The player begins with one district, then manages a portfolio of towns and cities over time. Buildings complete persistently, policies alter system behavior, events demand trade-offs and national programmes require coordinated investment across regions.

  • Target session: 3-5 minute check-ins supported by longer planning sessions.
  • Core mode: persistent management through a national map, city views and a strategic dashboard.
  • Time model: upgrades and initiatives continue between sessions, with clear scheduled outcomes.
  • Expansion: one district becomes several cities, then a state portfolio and national programmes.
Representative check-in

How persistent management feels

The player does not finish a world. They return to a living system, review what changed and make the next set of decisions.

01 · Review

Read the overnight report

See completed construction, resource changes, citizen feedback and emerging risks.

02 · Diagnose

Find the bottleneck

Compare cities and identify where mobility, power, skills or health constrain progress.

03 · Allocate

Commit the budget

Fund upgrades, move specialist capacity and reserve resources for national missions.

04 · Decide

Set policy priorities

Choose incentives and programmes that alter costs, growth, trust and sustainability.

05 · Respond

Resolve an event

Handle a water shortage, skills gap, migration spike or infrastructure failure.

06 · Compare

Measure contribution

Track state standing, national goals, friends and the next milestone toward 2047.

Bespoke feature model

Systems designed for a persistent nation

This route gives the initial client vision room to breathe, but every system below must be scoped and balanced from the ground up.

City portfolio

Specialize towns and cities, move resources between them and resolve local needs without losing the national view.

Policy and economy

Use a limited number of legible policy levers that affect tax income, services, growth, trust and sustainability.

Persistent construction

Schedule upgrades and programmes that complete over time, with acceleration earned through good planning rather than friction.

Cabinet assistant

Explain dependencies, forecast consequences and translate policy language into age-appropriate choices and feedback.

State competition

Compare states on balanced public metrics, avoiding a single wealth score that rewards only aggressive growth.

National events

Run shared missions, anniversary moments and time-limited goals that let individual progress contribute to collective outcomes.

Technical architecture

Web-first, mobile-ready, service-backed

Three.js provides the rendered world, not the whole product. The game also requires a deterministic simulation layer, persistent services, content configuration and a native wrapper strategy.

Recommended validation: prototype one city view, one cross-sector dependency and one persistent return session before committing to the full architecture.

Decision framework

Two credible products, not two skins

The routes use the same budget envelope differently. Variant 1 buys more content and deadline confidence. Variant 2 buys closer vision fidelity but requires a narrower first release.

Our recommendation: prototype Variant 1 first.

It is the most defensible route to a polished anniversary product. The 3-4 week MPP should also test one persistent dashboard screen. If that screen proves more compelling than the regional match, the project can deliberately move to Variant 2 before full production rather than discovering the mismatch later.

Release commitment

A concrete deadline can carry a concrete remedy.

NOXGAMES is open to fixing the final release deadline in the development contract and agreeing a price reduction if NOXGAMES misses that deadline.

Typical range: a negotiated 10-20% reduction for a missed final delivery deadline.
Defined in advance: exact deadline, grace period, measurement point and reduction percentage.
Protected scope: approved change requests or expanded requirements move the schedule through written change control.
Shared dependencies: client approval delays, missing inputs and other agreed exclusions are handled explicitly in the final contract.
Illustrative commercial structure only. Payment due dates, tax treatment, acceptance procedure, intellectual-property transfer, deadline remedies, exclusions and liability caps will be defined in the executed agreements.
